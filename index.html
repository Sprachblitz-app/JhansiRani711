🐻 Meet Sprachblitz

🐻

Your Learning Bear

Sprachblitz is powered by a friendly bear mascot who guides you through interactive German lessons, gamified grammar challenges, and real-world vocabulary. Built with React, Firebase, and 487 native German audio phrases.

🐻

Meet Bruno

Your German learning companion. Bruno's room is where your journey begins. "Guten Abend! Ich bin Bruno. Willkommen in meinem Zimmer!" Start your daily lessons and track progress with gamified rewards.

⚡

Daily Blitz Challenge

5 quick questions every day at your level. Earn XP and build streaks. "Start today's Blitz — up to +52 XP". Gamified learning keeps you coming back for more.

🎮

Free Practice Mode

Play grammar games, word matching, and article challenges (der/die/das). No pressure, no time limits. "Play a game or browse any topic. Nothing is locked here." Learn what YOU want when YOU want.

📝

Grammar Mastery

13 essential grammar topics including articles, pronouns, and verb forms. "Goethe A1 Unified Grammar Curriculum Matrix" — Core syntax rules, contextual formulation usage, and morphological tracking for true mastery.

🔊

Listening Lab + Mock Exams

Listen to 487 native German phrases with transcripts. Practice with official Goethe-Institut format exams (A1, A2, B1, B2). All four skills: Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking. Get exam-ready in your own time.
